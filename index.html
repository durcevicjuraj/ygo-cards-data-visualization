Data Visualization of Yu-Gi-Oh! Cards

This web application provides interactive visualization of data about Yu-Gi-Oh! cards. The goal is to provide players and analysts with a quick and easy insight into the strength, popularity and ban status of cards.

About

  • Interactive data visualization of over 13,000 Yu-Gi-Oh! cards.
  • Compare two cards using a radar chart (ATK, DEF, Level).
  • View the top 30 most popular cards based on views, upvotes, or downvotes.
  • Filter cards by type, race, attribute, archetype, and more.
  • Built with D3.js for dynamic charts and smooth transitions.
